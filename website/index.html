Restaurant Financial Intelligence

CEO Financial Performance & Control Review | 2023–2026

● Executive Management View
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Revenue generation is substantial, but financial reporting and control weaknesses require immediate management attention.

Analysis of recorded sales, receipts, payments, revenue by business unit and financial-control indicators identifies a strong revenue base alongside significant transaction-traceability and reconciliation weaknesses.

Key financial indicators

RECEIPTS ₦2.917bn Total recorded receipts
PAYMENTS ₦2.116bn Total recorded payments

BUSINESS PERFORMANCE

Where revenue is coming from

01

Restaurant is the core revenue engine

Restaurant contributes approximately 42.4% of recorded sales.

02

Revenue is concentrated

Restaurant and Club/Lounge together contribute approximately 66.5% of recorded sales.

03

Multiple business units contribute

Revenue is generated across Restaurant, Club/Lounge, Bar, Bakery and Greela operations.

FINANCIAL CONTROL RISK

Where management is exposed

19,146 Payments without Payee
33,605 Receipts without Reference
24,159 Receipts without Description
₦5.74m Positive Receivables

Missing Payees and receipt references are control weaknesses affecting transaction traceability and reconciliation. They are not, on their own, evidence of fraud.

CRITICAL ACCOUNTING WARNING

Recorded expenses collapse from April 2026

Recorded expenses fall sharply from approximately ₦58.5m in March 2026 to ₦2.1m in April, followed by further reductions in May–August.

This produces apparently exceptional profit margins, but those margins should not be treated as reliable profitability evidence until April 2026 onward has been reconciled.

CEO ACTION PLAN

What management should do first

01 — Reconcile April 2026 onward

Reconcile payments, expenses, receipts and bank/cash records from April 2026 before relying on reported profitability.

02 — Restore complete expense recording

Ensure all business expenses are recorded consistently and mapped to appropriate categories.

03 — Strengthen transaction controls

Make Payee, receipt reference and supporting descriptions mandatory for relevant transactions.

04 — Reclassify major “Other” payments

Review the largest unclassified payments to improve management visibility over spending.

05 — Introduce monthly bank reconciliation

Establish a documented monthly reconciliation process covering bank, cash, receipts and payments.

06 — Strengthen food & beverage controls

Monitor purchasing and consumption against sales to improve control over the largest payment categories.

MANAGEMENT TAKEAWAY

Fix financial visibility before optimising reported profit.

The analysis indicates significant revenue activity and cash movement, but weaknesses in transaction traceability, reconciliation and expense recording reduce confidence in the reported financial picture.
